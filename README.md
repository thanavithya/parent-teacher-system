 ParentTeacher Connect – Parent-Teacher Portal

School Communication & Collaboration Platform

ParentTeacher Connect is a responsive web application designed to improve communication and collaboration between parents, teachers, and school administrators. It provides a centralized platform for monitoring student academic progress, attendance, fees, assignments, school events, and parent-teacher meetings.

✨ Features

🔐 Login & Registration – Demo authentication with Parent, Teacher, and Admin roles.

📊 Role-Based Dashboard – Personalized dashboards with important school updates.

👨‍👩‍👧 Parent Portal – View children's academic progress, attendance, fees, and assignments.

👨‍🏫 Teacher Portal – Manage assigned students, marks, attendance, and parent communication.

👑 Admin Dashboard – Manage users, announcements, events, and reports.

💬 Messaging System – Inbox, sent messages, conversations, and unread message tracking.

📚 Academic Progress – Subject-wise marks, grades, performance charts, and report cards.

📅 Attendance Tracking – Daily attendance, attendance history, and percentage calculations.

💰 Fee Management – View fee details, payment history, outstanding balances, and demo receipts.

📢 Events & Announcements – Share school news, examination updates, and reminders.

🤝 Meeting Scheduler – Request, approve, and manage parent-teacher meetings.

📝 Homework & Assignments – Create assignments, track submissions, and provide feedback.

🌟 Behavior Tracking – Maintain student feedback and behavior records.

🖼️ Photo Gallery – Display school events and activities.

📈 Reports & Exports – Generate PDF and CSV reports.

🌙 Dark Mode – Switch between light and dark themes.

📱 Responsive Design – Supports desktop, tablet, and mobile screens.
